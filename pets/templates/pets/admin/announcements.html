Architectural Note: Platform Announcements vs Automated Notifications

Platform Announcements are platform-wide broadcasts (e.g. "Adoption Festival Drive", "Scheduled Maintenance", "New Policy Notice") targeted at audience segments. Transactional notifications (e.g., "Adoption Approved", "Payment Receipt") are managed separately via the 🔔 Notification Center.
